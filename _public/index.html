Nevergrove

Four Variants

Each one comprised of 48 colors and focused on an accent color. An inverse accent is used for things that require your attention. This includes selected text, highlighted by the visual selection color.

Most of the palette stays the same between variants, only background colors differ in order to maintain consistecy while giving each variant its own atmosphere.

Design Goals

Key ideas I've kept in mind while designing the palette:

  • Colorful Contrast: Nevergrove provides the calmness of desaturated backgrounds proved great by other palettes, while tuning up the intensity of foreground colors. Colors neither too pale nor too bright, vibrant but not overwhelming.
  • Avoid Transparency: Transparency offers huge possibilities, but I find more often that not it also makes themes inconsistent. A palette should foster creativity through constraints.

No AI was used in the creation of the Nevergrove palette, this site, or any of the official Nevergrove themes.
